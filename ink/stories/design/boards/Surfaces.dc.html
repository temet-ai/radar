06 · surfaces & layers

Depth without pixels.

A terminal has no shadows and no blur, but it has grounds. radar stacks five of them, darkest at the back, and Ink can now composite one layer over another: the screen behind a menu dims, the menu throws a one-cell shadow, and the thing that has your keys wears the ramp.

Overlays

A command palette over the Repositories screen. Everything behind drops to a scrim (each cell's colour mixed 75% toward bg.base), the palette sits on bg.overlay with a one-cell shadow under its right and bottom edges, and the letters that matched are lit in the accent.

With nothing typed, the palette shows a short discovery list: the chosen item's actions first, then the places you visit most. Each command's key sits on the right.

Menus, confirms and help use the same layer. Each opens with the popup sweep (07 · motion) and esc always closes the top one.

Notices

Quick, small news a write leaves behind. A notice is one row at the bottom-right of the panel above the status line, inside its border, not a floating box. Its tinted ground drains cell by cell as its time runs out; a newer notice stands below and the older one dims above it. It carries at most one key, underlined in its word, and esc clears them all.

Information stays about 5 s; an error stays about 10 s or until a key. Every notice is kept in a message history, opened with one key from anywhere.

Panel states

Every panel can be loading, empty or broken, and says so in its own space. Loading shows the shape of what is coming and shimmers; empty says why and what to do; an error says what answered, never a parser's complaint.
