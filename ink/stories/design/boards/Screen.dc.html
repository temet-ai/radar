08 · the screen

Repositories, drawn by Ink.

Every sheet at once, at 160 × 40 cells. Each pane title carries its jump number; the list keeps its cached rows while it syncs, with the spinner in its title. Part marks and names in the top bar with their keys underlined, no counts; the focused panel in the ramp; one tree where folded rows end in ⋯ n, and the five row states; square tinted chips; hints that read as words. The detail pane has one structure: a head on the heading shade, a column of facts with their labels aligned, then sections divided by named rules, each starting at the same indent, with ages pushed to the right edge.
