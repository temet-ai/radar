07 · motion

Motion explains a change.

Ink's motion engine ticks every 30 ms, and only while an effect runs. Each effect answers one question: where did this come from, what just changed, is anything happening. They run on whole cells, the way a terminal draws. Any key, click, wheel or paste cancels them at once, so motion never stands between you and the next key. The demos below loop; switch them between real speed and four times slower with the speed tweak.

Levels

[theme] motion = "full" | "subtle" | "off"
full every effect on this sheet
subtle screen fade, popup, changed rows, notices, focus
off radar draws as it does without motion

Rules

 any key, click, wheel or paste ends every effect
 pointer movement does not
 three slow frames in a row: motion off, and it says so
 off over SSH and on 16 colours or none
 the frame after an effect is the frame without it
