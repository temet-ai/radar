Slots, not hex codes.

A part never holds a colour. It names a slot, as a spec such as status.warning bold, and the theme fills it. The default palette is SilkCircuit Neon; anyone can override a slot in [theme] slots, and a part can declare its own slots with defaults. Every slot has a 256-colour and a 16-colour stand-in, so a screen keeps its meaning on any terminal.

Ramps & tints

Truecolour lets one cell differ from the next. Two derived colours, both computed from the slots so a user palette carries through.

The glow ramp

glow.purple → accent.tertiary → glow.cyan · one step per cell

Used for one thing at a time: the focused panel's border, the wordmark, a progress bar that is the point of the screen. On 256 colours and below it collapses to accent.primary.
