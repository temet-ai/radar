Five rules

Everything on the following sheets follows from these. When a choice is unclear, decide by them.

01

The cell is the pixel

Every mark lands on the grid. Nothing half a cell wide, nothing that only looks right in a browser.

02

Colour carries meaning

Status colours only for status. Accents for focus and action. Glow is for one thing at a time.

03

Rich where it informs

Icons on headings, chips, people, states and tabs; several to a row when each says something.

04

Motion explains change

It shows where a thing came from or that it changed. It never holds back a key.

05

Every layer degrades

truecolour to 256 to 16 to none; nerd to unicode to off; full to subtle to off; picture to text.
