04 · components

The kit, today and next.

Each part draws with the same small kit, so a chip in Slack is a chip in Jira. Every row on this sheet shows the component as radar draws it now, then the Ink version this guide proposes. Shapes stay square: a ground, a space each side, no round caps and no stripe down the left.

A short state, a count or a label that sits in a row. Tone decides the colour; the icon comes first.

Keys

A key is part of its word. The hint reads as the action, and the letter you press is underlined in it.

Key, then word: the eye reads "b" and "bulk" as two things.

in inknew

↑↓ move ⏎ open esc back

The word in secondary text with its key letter underlined in cyan. Name the action so its key is in it; keys with no letter (↑↓, ⏎, esc) stay a glyph before the word. The hint under the pointer takes the hover shade, as quit does here.
