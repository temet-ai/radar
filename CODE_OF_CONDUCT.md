# Code of conduct

radar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the
work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in a radar space is a problem, report it privately through GitHub:
on [temet-ai/radar](https://github.com/temet-ai/radar), open the **Security** tab and choose
**Report a vulnerability**, and say in the title that it is a conduct report. Only the
maintainers see these reports. We will look at every report, keep the reporter's identity
private, and act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private warning
up to a ban from the project.
